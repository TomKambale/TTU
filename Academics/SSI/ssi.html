Mathematics, Statistics and Physical Sciences Department

What We Do

We offer training leading to certificate, diploma, undergraduate and postgraduate in; Mathematics, Statistics, Information Technology and Computer Science. We also offer modules in CISCO. TTU is also the leading university in I.T. and Computer Science in East and Central Africa, the first within Kenya to have started I.T. and Computer Science. TTU department of Mathematics and Informatics is staffed by some of the best scholars in many fields ranging from statistics to software engineering.

Informatics and Computing Department

I&C Department

What We Do

The Informatics and Computing Department aims at producing top notch scholars and practitioners to offer competitive leadership in all aspects of Informatics and Communication Technology and Geo-informatics for the national and international marketplace. We cultivate positive attitude towards computing and its dynamics in a view to produce graduates capable of adapting to the dynamic trends of global markets while upholding the Taita Taveta University's core values, embraced within our academic programs.

Our Mission: To produce leaders and professionals in computing, Geo-informatics for advancing science and entrepreneurship for enhancing socio-economic and sustainable development.
