History of the Department

The department was established in 2011 when the college was a campus. The department has members who are highly skilled and motivated in procurement and supply chain activities.

Department Scope: Activities within the Procurement Department cover a wide range of functions such as maintaining a list of prequalified bidders, preparing and publishing procurement opportunities, coordinating evaluation of tenders and quotations, issuing bid documents, and conducting periodic and annual stock taking.

The department has evolved significantly since the university's establishment, implementing modern procurement systems and technologies to enhance efficiency and transparency in procurement management.
