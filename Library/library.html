About the Library

Supporting teaching, learning and research through quality information resources and world-class services.

Welcome to Taita Taveta University Library, located at Taita Taveta University main campus. The Library has the following sections: User Services, Technical Section, Digital Library and Reprographic Section.

We are reinventing our resources, services and systems to what a World Class Library can and aspire to be — anticipating and adapting to the changing needs of students and faculty.

Vision & Mission

Vision

Actively participate in teaching, learning and research by providing information resources and services to the University community.

Mission Statement

To develop, organize and preserve quality information resources in various formats to broaden teaching, learning and research activities and to meet the information needs of the University.

Strategic Goals

Four key priorities guiding our commitment to excellence in library services.

1

Quality Information Resources

To acquire, organize and maintain quality information resources in print and digital format to meet teaching and research needs of the University.

2

User-Focused Services

To provide user focused library service to meet needs and expectations of the user community.

3

Increase Library Usage

Increase library usage among the university community through awareness and accessibility improvements.

4

Organizational Development

To develop an organizational framework to enhance library administration and functions for efficient service delivery.

Important Information

Plagiarism Check Requirement: All Taita Taveta University students are required to present their academic papers for plagiarism check at the Digital Library.

Organization & Access

The Library of Congress Classification Scheme (LCCS) has been used to organize TTU information resources. KOHA Library Management System has been adopted in automation of Library functions.
