Discover our rich history, guiding philosophy, and commitment to excellence in education, research, and sustainable development

Explore Our Journey

Our History

TTU opened her gates in 2007 as Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT) Taita Taveta Campus and later in 2011 was upgraded to a Constituent until 7th October 2016 when it was given a charter to be a fully fledged university.

Due to her very attractive courses, the population in the University has risen to 2000 students undertaking Masters', Undergraduate, Diploma and Certificate programs. The University has a constant presence of international scholars through its established international linkages with reputable Universities across the world.

Taita Taveta University (TTU) programs are guided by its Mission and Vision, and is proud as a mining and mineral processing engineer's hub.

Our Guiding Principles

The foundation of our academic excellence and institutional values

Our Philosophy

Offering quality education, training, research, and outreach programs to empower communities for the improvement of their social well-being while assuring environmental sustainability.

Our Mission

The mission of the University is to be a University for research, education, teaching, and community services in management of mineral and natural resources.

Our Vision

The vision is to be an educated and transformed society in the sustainability of natural resources.

Our Academic Schools

Five specialized schools offering diverse programs in various fields of study

School of Mines and Engineering

Leading hub for mining and mineral processing engineering education and research in the region. State-of-the-art laboratories and industry partnerships.

School of Business, Economics and Social Sciences

Developing business leaders and social scientists equipped to address contemporary economic challenges through innovative thinking and research.

School of Science and Informatics

Advancing scientific knowledge and technological innovation through cutting-edge research and education in computing, IT, and pure sciences.

School of Agriculture, Earth and Environmental Sciences

Promoting sustainable agriculture and environmental conservation through science-based solutions and practical field training.

School of Education

Training quality educators and educational leaders to transform teaching and learning practices across Kenya and beyond.

Strategic Pillars

The foundation of our institutional strategy for growth and excellence

The strategy house presents the strategic focus for TTU showing the key pillars (Key Result Areas) which the strategy is built on. At the apex is the Vision and Mission supported by three main pillars of Research, Academics, and Outreach. Institutional capacity, Resource mobilization and Corporate governance form the foundational pillars for the strategic plan.

Academics

Excellence in teaching, learning, and curriculum development to produce highly competent graduates ready for the global market.

Research

Innovative research addressing local, regional, and global challenges, particularly in natural resource management and sustainable development.

Outreach

Community engagement and service that translates knowledge into practical solutions for societal development and empowerment.

Institutional Capacity

Developing human resources, infrastructure, and systems to support university operations and sustainable growth.

Resource Mobilization

Diversifying funding sources and optimizing resource utilization for sustainable development and financial independence.

Corporate Governance

Ensuring ethical leadership, transparency, and accountability in all university affairs and decision-making processes.

Our Location

A serene environment for learning and research

Serene Environment for Learning

Taita Taveta University (TTU) is a fast-growing Institution located in Taita Taveta County on Voi-Mwatate road, about 8 kilometers from the Nairobi-Mombasa highway.

It lies in a serene environment bordering Tsavo East National Park on the east and Tsavo West National Park on the North West, the home of the Red elephants. This unique location provides an ideal setting for research and study in environmental sciences, wildlife management, and sustainable tourism.
