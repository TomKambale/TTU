Who We Are

A voluntary organization that aims to further lifelong partnership between Taita Taveta University and members of the Association.

OUR VISION

To become a strong external community of committed alumni, promoting the image of the University of excellence in training, research and innovation.

OUR MISSION

To create a pool of leaders and professionals in the field of engineering, Business, Agriculture, Science and technology and engage in collective action of advancement of TTU at national, regional and international level.

Alumni Association Executive Board

The Alumni Executive Board oversees the Alumni Association's efforts to advance and support alumni, students and the university. As the Taita Taveta University Alumni Association's governing body, the Board is composed of registered alumni whose demonstrated service and leadership display a strong understanding and commitment to our mission.

Mr. Richard W. Kibengo

Chairman, Taita Taveta University Alumni Association

"On behalf of the Taita Taveta Alumni Association Executive Board and as Chairman of the Alumni Association, I welcome you to our online platform!

One of our goals is to preserve a mutually beneficial, enduring relationship among alums and with the University. As your representatives, we are constantly seeking meaningful ways to embrace your needs and unite our efforts.

I urge you to become an active alum and share in the exciting activities and opportunities this organization has to offer. There are a variety of ways you can show appreciation and play a part in the success of this association. This includes attending events, cheering for our teams, staying connected to each other and the University, and of course, offering your time and gifts to help TTU students. Through this site you can: get involved with the Alumni Association, read about inspiring alumni accomplishments, find out about upcoming alumni activities, learn about on-campus events, volunteer and much more.

Wherever you are, at whatever stage of life you are in, there's something here for you. I encourage you to share and use this site as one way to engage with Taita Taveta University Alumni Association and maintain your personal connection to the University.

We are excited about the direction and future of the Taita Taveta University Alumni Association and look forward to serving and sharing information with you, throughout the year. Choose to support the Alumni Association."

Mr. Richard W. Kibengo

Chairman, Taita Taveta University Alumni Association
