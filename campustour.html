Frequently Asked Questions

How long will the tour take?

The tour takes three to four hours. This includes visits to academic buildings, student facilities, and time for Q&A with admissions representatives.

Is there any parking nearby?

Yes, we have visitor parking available at the Admissions Office. Please inform us when booking if you will require parking so we can reserve a spot for you.

Can I leave my bags or luggage at meeting point?

Yes, we have secure storage facilities at the Admissions Office where you can leave your bags during the tour. Please inform us in advance if you plan to use this service.

Will the tour be cancelled due to weather?

Tours proceed in most weather conditions. In case of extreme weather, we will contact you to reschedule. Indoor alternatives are available for heavy rain.

Can I bring guests on the tour?

Yes, you can bring up to 2 guests. Please indicate the number of participants when booking your tour. For larger groups, please contact us directly.

Is the tour accessible for people with disabilities?

Yes, our campus is accessible. Please let us know about any accessibility requirements when booking so we can ensure your tour accommodates your needs.

Complete Your Visit

To learn more about the specific admissions process for your program, we recommend that you arrange to visit a specific graduate or professional school. We suggest that you connect with your specific school or department of interest ahead of time.

  • Contact the department directly to arrange meetings with faculty
  • Schedule appointments with academic advisors
  • Attend a class in your field of interest (with prior arrangement)

Foreign Visitors

The University has a constant presence of international scholars through its established international linkages with reputable Universities across the world.

We provide:

  • Multilingual tour guides (available upon request)
  • Information on visa requirements and international student services
  • Connections with current international students
  • Guidance on housing options for international students
