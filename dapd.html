Directorate of Administration, Planning and Development

Facilitating optimal utilization of human and non-human resources to realize the University's strategic objectives

Established in 2018

Overview

In order to address the challenges of administration, planning and development, Taita Taveta University established the Directorate of Administration Planning and Development (D, APD) in 2018.

The Directorate facilitates the optimal utilization of human and non-human resources in an effort to realize the objectives as envisaged in the University Master Plan, Strategic Plan (2023-2028) and Citizen Service Delivery Charter.

Through strategic planning and efficient administration, the directorate ensures that the university's resources are effectively aligned with its mission and vision for sustainable growth and development.

Specific Functions of the Directorate

The Directorate is charged with specific functions which include:

Recruitment & Placement

Overseeing the recruitment, placement, induction and training of university staff to ensure the right talent is in the right position.

Promotions & Discipline

Managing staff promotions and disciplinary matters in accordance with university policies and labor laws.

Personnel Registry

Maintaining accurate and secure personnel records and registry systems for all university staff.

Facilities Management

Management and maintenance of the University facilities to support academic and administrative functions.

Quality Management

Overseeing the University Quality Management System to ensure continuous improvement in service delivery.

Accommodation & Catering

Provision of quality and affordable accommodation and catering services for students and staff.

Departments

The Administration, Planning and Development Directorate is composed of the following Departments:

Department of Facilities

Responsible for the maintenance and management of all university facilities and infrastructure.

Department of Catering and Accommodation

Ensuring quality and affordable accommodation and catering services for the university community.

Department of Planning Development and Central Services

Strategic planning and development initiatives to support university growth and objectives.

Department of Human Resources

Managing recruitment, staff development, promotions, and personnel registry services.
