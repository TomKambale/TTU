Finance Department

Providing statutory and financial information for decision-making and controlling the financial activities of TTU

Administration, Finance and Planning Division

Department Leadership

Mr. Peter Kisombe

Director of Finance

The Finance Department of TTU is responsible for providing statutory and financial information for decision-making and for the control of the financial activities of TTU.

Mr. Kisombe leads a dedicated team of professional staff in the department, overseeing all financial operations and ensuring compliance with TTU regulations and policies.

What We Do

The following activities of the Finance Department are intended to define its operational objectives in line with the relevant TTU regulations and policies:

Financial Books of Accounts

Preparation and maintenance of financial books of accounts to ensure accurate and complete financial records.

Source Documents Management

Preparation of source documents including payment vouchers, invoices, receipts to facilitate management of receipts and expenditure activities.

Statutory Reports & Records

Preparation and maintenance of proper statutory reports/records in compliance with regulatory requirements.

Pay and Benefit System

Preparation and management of the Pay and Benefit System for all university staff.

Annual Report Preparation

Preparation and publication of statutory statements (Annual Report) reflecting the university's financial performance.

Management Reports

Preparation of periodic management reports to support informed decision-making by university leadership.

Budgeting & Control

Budgeting and Budgetary Control of financial activities including preparation of five-year business plan with financial targets and performance indicators.

Assets & Records Management

Proper custody and management of assets and records to ensure their security and proper utilization.

Cash Flow & Investments

Cash flow and investments management to optimize the university's financial resources.

Income Generating Projects

Facilitating identification and implementation of income generating projects/programmes and preparing relevant financial reporting documents to external funding agencies.

Loans and Grants Management

Management of loans and grants to ensure proper utilization and compliance with terms and conditions.

Other Financial Duties

Any other duties in line with the TTU regulations and policies to support the university's financial health.

History of the Department

Department Structure

The Finance Department is part of the Administration, Finance and Planning (AFP) Division of Taita Taveta University under the leadership of the Deputy Principal (AFP), with the Principal/CEO as the Accounting Officer.

It is headed by the Acting Finance Officer assisted by the Acting Deputy Finance Officer, who work with a dedicated team of professional staff in the department.

Department Functions

Activities within the Finance Department cover a wide range of functions such as basic bookkeeping, providing information, assisting managers in making strategic decisions, the day-to-day transactional accounting for the Institution and preparing the financial statements.

The department ensures that all financial operations are conducted in accordance with established policies, procedures, and regulatory requirements while supporting the strategic objectives of the university.
