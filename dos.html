What We Do

The Office of the Dean of Students is dedicated to enhancing the student experience at TTU by providing comprehensive support services, fostering personal development, and creating a vibrant campus community. We serve as advocates for students and work to ensure their holistic development during their time at the university.

Accommodation & Catering Services

Providing comfortable, safe, and affordable accommodation options for students, along with quality catering services that meet diverse dietary needs and preferences.

Co-curricular Programs

Establishing strong co-curricular programmes in sports and games, clubs and societies to promote talent development, teamwork, and leadership skills.

Guidance & Counseling

Ensuring availability of strong guidance and counseling programmes and providing timely counseling services for academic, personal, and psychological support.

Career Development

Creating awareness of career opportunities to final year and other students through workshops, career fairs, and industry connections.

Mentorship Programs

Encouraging mentorship, working with university students and secondary schools surrounding the University, to enhance transition rates to institutions of higher learning.

Student Activities & Welfare

Coordinating students' activities, relations and welfare to ensure a balanced and fulfilling university experience for all students.

Student Association Elections

Serving as Returning Officer for students Association Elections, ensuring free, fair, and transparent electoral processes.

Prevention of Alcohol & Drug Abuse

Implementing programs and initiatives to prevent alcohol and drug abuse among students through education, awareness, and support services.

History of the Department

The Students Welfare Department was established to assist students and staff on moral, spiritual, personal, and relationship issues affecting them in the University. This has grown over time as the population has continued to grow.

Initially operating with limited staff and resources, the department has expanded its services to meet the evolving needs of an increasingly diverse student population. From its humble beginnings, the office has developed into a comprehensive student support system addressing academic, social, emotional, and developmental aspects of student life.

Our commitment has always been to create a supportive environment where every student can thrive academically, socially, and personally.

Today, the Office of the Dean of Students continues to evolve, implementing new programs and services to address contemporary student needs while maintaining its core mission of supporting student welfare and development. The department remains committed to fostering an inclusive, supportive, and engaging campus community for all TTU students.
