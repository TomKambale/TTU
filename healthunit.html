History of the Department

2008

Establishment

The Health Unit department was started at the opening of the Campus as one roomed department. It was later shifted east of the administration building which currently serves as the Estate department.

During this time the department had one staff, a Clinical Officer who worked during the day and on call at night. All diseases were treated clinically because we did not have a medical laboratory then and for those diagnosis which needed laboratory investigation were sent to the District Hospital.

2009

Expansion and Development

In September, we received a Laboratory Technologist but due to lack of laboratory equipment the laboratory became operational the following month.

On October 2009 the department received two more staffs (a Clinical Officer and a Nurse). At this point the department became operational 24hrs a day, 7 days a week.

2013

Leadership Enhancement

In September 2013, a Medical Officer was hired and currently heads the department. Other Staffs were later recruited: Pharmaceutical Technologist, Records Officer, Nurse and Cleaner/Messenger.

Present

Current Operations

Apart from the permanent Staffs, we have Clinical Officers and Nurses on locum basis. The department now includes multiple sub-departments and provides comprehensive healthcare services to the university community.
