What We Do

Our mandate as a department is to:

  • Develop and implement ICT policies, procedures, and standards to ensure efficient and secure operations.
  • Manage ICT projects, including the deployment of new technologies and systems to support the university’s academic, administrative, and research functions.
  • Provide reliable and innovative ICT services to students, faculty, and staff.
  • Offer user support and training to enhance digital literacy and ensure effective utilization of ICT resources.
  • Ensure the security, integrity, and availability of the University’s ICT systems and data.
  • Identify and integrate emerging technologies to enhance University operations and services.

Key Functions

ICT Infrastructure Management

Overseeing the university’s network, servers, and cloud systems.

Project Management

Leading ICT projects from planning to execution, ensuring timely and within-budget delivery.

User Support and Training

Providing technical assistance and training programs to empower users with ICT tools and systems.

Cybersecurity and Data Protection

Implementing measures to safeguard the university’s digital assets and sensitive information.

Service Management

Delivering and maintaining ICT services such as email, learning management systems, and enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems.
