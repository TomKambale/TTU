Department Functions & Responsibilities

The Corporate & Legal Affairs Department provides comprehensive legal services and corporate governance support to Taita Taveta University. Our mandate encompasses legal advisory, contract management, compliance oversight, and corporate governance to ensure the University operates within the legal framework and achieves its strategic objectives.

Contract Management

Drafting, reviewing, and negotiating all University contracts, agreements, and legal documents to protect institutional interests.

Legal Advisory Services

Providing expert legal advice to University management, Senate, Council, and all departments on various legal matters.

Compliance & Risk Management

Ensuring University compliance with all applicable laws, regulations, and policies, and managing legal risks.

Dispute Resolution

Managing litigation matters and providing mediation services to resolve disputes efficiently and cost-effectively.

Corporate Governance

Supporting the University Council and management in implementing sound corporate governance practices.

Intellectual Property Management

Protecting and managing the University's intellectual property rights, including patents, trademarks, and copyrights.
