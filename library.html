Welcome to Our Library

The Taita Taveta University Library has the following sections: User services, Technical section, Digital Library and Reprographic section. We are reinventing our resources, services and systems to what a World Class Library can and aspire to be. We are doing this as a way of anticipating and adapting to the changing needs of students and faculty.

We are committed to support all teaching and research activities at TTU through delivery of high quality services to all information seekers. We invite you to share our passion for lifelong learning to join the Library in achieving her vision.

Your continuous feedback is essential in helping us reinvent a Library for the future.

Organization & Access to Information Resources

The Library of Congress Classification Scheme (LCCS) has been used to organize TTU information resources. KOHA Library Management System has been adopted in automation of Library functions.

Vision

Actively participate in teaching, learning and research by providing information resources and services to the University community.

Mission Statement

To develop, organize and preserve quality information resources in various formats to broaden teaching, learning and research activities and to meet information needs of the University.

Strategic Goals

1

Quality Information Resources

To acquire, organize and maintain quality information resources in print and digital format to meet teaching and research needs of the University College.

2

User-Focused Services

To provide user focused library service to meet needs and expectations of the user community.

3

Increase Library Usage

Increase library usage among university community.

4

Organizational Framework

To develop an organizational framework to enhance library administration and functions.

Important Information to all TTU Students

  • All Taita Taveta University students are required to present their academic papers for plagiarism check at the Digital Library.
  • Ensure you provide your email address for feedback.
  • Only TTU students are allowed to submit papers for plagiarism checking.
