About the Library

Welcome to Taita Taveta University Library, located at Taita Taveta University main campus. The Library has the following sections: User services, Technical section, Digital Library and Reprographic section.

We are reinventing our resources, services and systems to what a World Class Library can and aspire to be. We are doing this as a way of anticipating and adapting to the changing needs of students and faculty.

Vision

Actively participate in teaching, learning and research by providing information resources and services to the University community.

Mission Statement

To develop, organize and preserve quality information resources in various formats to broaden teaching, learning and research activities and to meet information needs of the University.

Strategic Goals

1

Quality Information Resources

To acquire, organize and maintain quality information resources in print and digital format to meet teaching and research needs of the University College.

2

User-Focused Services

To provide user focused library service to meet needs and expectations of the user community.

3

Increase Library Usage

Increase library usage among university community through awareness and accessibility improvements.

4

Organizational Development

To develop an organizational framework to enhance library administration and functions for efficient service delivery.
