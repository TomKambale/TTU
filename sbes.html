Business and Management Sciences Department

BMS Department

What We Do

The Department of Business and Management Sciences (BMS) is one of the departments in the School of Business, Economics and Social Sciences (SBESS). The department offers teaching training programmes at Certificate, Diploma, Bachelors, Masters and Doctoral levels. We also offer short professional courses in the areas of accounting and procurement.

The department is committed to train highly skilled manpower in the fields of Business, information technology and management through offering the following courses:

Undergraduate Programmes:

  • Bachelor of Commerce (BCOM) with specializations in:
    • Accounting
    • Finance
    • Marketing
    • Human Resource Management
  • Bachelor of Purchasing and Supplies management (BPSM)
  • Bachelor of Business Information Technology (BBIT)

Postgraduate Programmes:

  • Masters of Business Administration (MBA) with specialization in:
    • Accounting
    • Finance
    • Strategic Management
    • Marketing
    • Human Resource Management
    • Natural Resource Management
    • Operations Management

Diploma and Certificate Programmes:

  • Diploma in Purchasing and Supplies Management (DPSM)
  • Diploma in County Governance (DCG)
  • Certificate in Purchasing and Supplies Management (CPSM)
  • Certificate in County Governance (CCG)

Currently the department has finalized the development of Doctor of Philosophy programme with specializations in Accounting, Finance, Strategic Management, Marketing, Human Resource management and Operations management. This will commence soon.

Department of Economics and Entrepreneurship

EESS Department

What We Do

Department of Economics, Entrepreneurship and Social Sciences (EESS) is one of the departments in the School of Business, Economics and Social Sciences (SBESS). It was established in 2018 following the splitting of the former department of Business Studies and Economics following the introduction of Bachelor of Science in Economics.

The department provides teaching services to other departments in the university in the following areas: Economics, Development studies, Communication Skills, and Entrepreneurship Skills.

Department of Arts and Social Sciences

ASOS Department

Vision

Empowered individuals and communities through arts, humanities and social sciences for positive social transformation.

Mission

To provide inclusive, interdisciplinary education, training and research in the arts, humanities and social sciences that equips individuals with the knowledge, skills and creativity for social transformation.

Brief about the Department

The department of Arts and Social Sciences (A&SS) in the School of Business Economics and Social Sciences, offers courses that are tailored to respond to contemporary realities and the transformation of society. Our curriculum is designed to deepen understanding of our contemporary world by encouraging an inquiry into the persistent legacies of the past that shape our present and evolving futures.

The department aims at producing individuals who are capable of reflecting on cross-cutting social, cultural and political issues, understand complex societal dynamics and initiate transformative dialogue about the human condition. As such, A&SS offers students a cutting-edge opportunity to study how individuals and groups interact, social formations, societal transformation, the development of societal norms and values, and an inquiry into and engagement in policy making. Learners attain tools to navigate complex issues, promote social cohesion, envision and action a better future.
