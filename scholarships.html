County Grant Programs

Taita Taveta County Government offers several grant programs for local students pursuing higher education. These grants are designed to support students from the county in accessing university education.

Eligibility typically requires proof of residency in Taita Taveta County, demonstrated financial need, and maintenance of satisfactory academic progress.

Government Scholarships

The Kenyan government, through various ministries and agencies, provides scholarship opportunities for university students. These include the Higher Education Loans Board (HELB), Constituency Development Fund (CDF) bursaries, and Ministry of Education scholarships.

These programs support students based on academic merit, financial need, and alignment with national development priorities.

Scholarship Requirements

As long as you're in need and you meet the following requirements:

  • Full-time Student Status: You must be registered as a full-time student at Taita Taveta University.
  • Timely Financial Aid Application: You must have applied for financial aid by the specified deadline for the academic year.
  • Satisfactory Academic Progress: You must be meeting the Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP) standards as defined by the university.
  • Consistent Financial Need: You must have approximately the same amount of financial need that you had in prior years (for renewal scholarships).
  • Complete Documentation: You must submit all required documentation, including financial statements, academic records, and personal statements as required by the specific scholarship.

Guidance and Advice

The University's high quality training is affirmed by the graduates as they hit the job market with their professionalism, innovativeness and ethical conduct making them stand out from the rest.

The hospitable and friendly community around Taita Taveta welcomes students from all walks of life to study at the Home of Ideas. Our financial aid advisors are available to help you navigate the scholarship application process and identify opportunities that match your profile.

We recommend that students begin their scholarship search early, maintain strong academic records, and carefully follow application instructions for each funding opportunity.

Pro Tip:

Apply for multiple scholarships! Even smaller awards can add up to significantly reduce your educational expenses.
