Committee Overview

The Sealing, Resource Mobilization and Academic Affairs Committee is a standing committee of the University Council established to provide specialized oversight and guidance in its area of responsibility. The committee plays a critical role in ensuring that the University's operations align with its strategic objectives and governance policies.

The committee consists of three Council members appointed based on their expertise and experience relevant to the committee's mandate. The Chairperson of the committee is responsible for leading meetings and ensuring the committee fulfills its responsibilities effectively.

Committee Mandate

Primary Purpose and Authority

The Sealing, Resource Mobilization and Academic Affairs Committee is established by the University Council with the authority to oversee and make recommendations on matters related to [committee's area of focus]. The committee's mandate includes ensuring compliance with relevant policies, regulations, and best practices in its domain.

Specific Responsibilities

Reviewed Terms of Reference for the Sealing, Resource Mobilization and Academic Affairs Committee as follows:

Sealing

To consider and authorize on behalf of Council the application of the University Seal on academic Documents and any other document as the case may require and ensure security of the documents.

Resource Mobilization

Approve Policies to Guide resource mobilisation To provide strategic direction and oversight for resource mobilization efforts in TTU Ensure alignment of resource mobilization activities with TTU’s mission and strategic goals and compliance with legal and ethical standards; Support the development and implementation of resource mobilization initiatives Monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of resource mobilization strategies and initiatives.

Academic Compliance

Oversight on compliance with CUE guidelines. Receive and consider reports from Senate and make recommendations to Council on Academic, Research, innovation, and community outreach Matters. To develop and review statutes for the University To deal with any other matter that may be referred

The committee operates within the powers delegated by the University Council and reports regularly to the Council on its activities, findings, and recommendations.

Committee Members

The committee comprises three Council members appointed based on their expertise and experience relevant to the committee's mandate.

Key Functions

Strategic Oversight

Provide guidance on strategic direction and monitor implementation of approved plans within the committee's area of responsibility.

Policy Review

Review existing policies and recommend new policies to ensure alignment with best practices and regulatory requirements.

Risk Management

Identify potential risks and recommend appropriate mitigation strategies to protect the University's interests.

Resource Oversight

Review financial and resource allocation to ensure efficient and effective use within the committee's domain.

Compliance Monitoring

Monitor compliance with relevant laws, regulations, and internal policies, recommending corrective actions as needed.

Performance Evaluation

Assess performance against established metrics and targets, recommending improvements to enhance outcomes.
