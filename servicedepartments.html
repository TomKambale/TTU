Finance Department

Ensuring financial accountability and strategic resource management

Mr. Peter Kisombe

Acting Finance Officer

The Finance Department of TTU is responsible for providing statutory and financial information for decision-making and for the control of the financial activities of TTU.

What We Do

The following activities of the Finance Department are intended to define its operational objectives in line with the relevant TTU regulations and policies and include:

  • Preparation and maintenance of financial books of accounts.
  • Preparation of source documents including but not limited to payment vouchers, invoices, receipts, which are required to facilitate the management of receipts and expenditure activities.
  • Preparation and maintenance of proper statutory reports/records.
  • Preparation and management of the Pay and Benefit System.
  • Preparation and publication of statutory statements (Annual Report).
  • Preparation of Periodic management reports.
  • Budgeting and Budgetary Control of financial activities. This shall include preparation of five-year business plan containing financial targets and performance indicators.
  • Proper custody and management of assets and records.
  • Cash flow and investments management.
  • Facilitating Identification and implementation of income generating projects/programmes and preparing relevant financial reporting documents to external funding agencies.
  • Management of loans and grants.
  • Any other duties in line with the TTU regulations and policies.

History of the Department

The Finance Department is part of the Administration, Finance and Planning (AFP) Division of Taita Taveta University under the leadership of the Deputy Principal (AFP), with the Principal/CEO as the Accounting Officer. It is headed by the Acting Finance Officer assisted by the Acting Deputy Finance Officer, who work with a dedicated team of professional staff in the department.

Activities within the Finance Department cover a wide range of functions such as basic bookkeeping, providing information, assisting managers in making strategic decisions, the day-to-day transactional accounting for the Institution and preparing the financial statements.

ICT Department

The central hub for managing and supporting university ICT infrastructure

Mandate of the Department

The Department of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) is the central hub for managing and supporting the university's ICT infrastructure, systems, and services. Our mandate is to:

  • Develop and implement ICT policies, procedures, and standards to ensure efficient and secure operations.
  • Manage ICT projects, including the deployment of new technologies and systems to support the university's academic, administrative, and research functions.
  • Provide reliable and innovative ICT services to students, faculty, and staff.
  • Offer user support and training to enhance digital literacy and ensure effective utilization of ICT resources.
  • Ensure the security, integrity, and availability of the University's ICT systems and data.

Catering Department

Providing quality food services to the university community

What We Do

  • Preparation, production and service of meals
  • Menu compilation, interpretation and economic use of food materials
  • Requisition and control of all food ingredients as per the menu
  • Developing function and conference as well as excellent outside catering services
  • Coordinating and executing cleanliness and hygiene procedures during operations
  • Handling customer, student, and staff complaints and providing feedback to the public relations office
  • We offer event catering such as outside catering services, conference, seminars, wedding receptions, and cocktail parties

About Our Services

The Catering department is among the premier departments in the university because from the institution's inception in 2007 the kitchen stood tall in providing services to the students, staff and the community around.

Progressively the department has been growing from a one-headed unit with few staff and providing services, only at the Mess for all people, to now a more extensive service provision from the mess catering for bonafide students at subsidized rates.

Texas cafeteria caters for both students and staff and offers variety of meals and can also hosts special functions e.g. birthday, end of year parties religious and club gatherings.

Makuti Restaurant (staff cafeteria) an ample candle lit wind, serene environment, a view of Sagalla hills. A thatched house reflecting the coastal tradition in the kind of delicacy it offers. It offers services to the university community and visitors from late breakfast, mid morning tea, lunch, and afternoon tea. In addition we also offer catering services for departmental bookings.

We strive to build a brand image in the hospitality industry with improved services from our trained and experienced staff.

History of the Department

The Health Unit department was started at the opening of the Campus as one roomed department. It was later shifted east of the administration a building which currently serves as the Estate department. During this time the department had one staff, a Clinical Officer who worked during the day and on call at night. All diseases were treated clinically because we did not have a medical laboratory then and for those diagnosis which needed laboratory investigation were sent to the District Hospital. It was until September that we received a Laboratory Technologist but due to lack of laboratory equipments the laboratory became operational the following month. On October 2009 the department received two more staffs (a Clinical Officer and a Nurse). At this point the department became operational 24hrs a day, 7 days a week. It has the following sub departments; records, pharmacy, outpatient consultation room, laboratory, drug store, treatment room, one male and one female observation rooms, wash room, and staffs office. In September 2013, a Medical Officer was hired and currently heads the department. Other Staffs were later recruited- Pharmaceutical Technologist, Records Officer, Nurse and Cleaner/ messenger. Apart from the permanent Staffs, we have Clinical Officers and Nurses on low cum basis.
